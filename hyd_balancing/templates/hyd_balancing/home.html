Optimize your heating system, save energy, and increase comfort. This tool guides you through the process of calculating the correct valve settings for your radiators.

Why is it important?

Hydraulic balancing ensures that every radiator receives the exact amount of heat it needs. Without it, some rooms may be too cold while others are too hot, leading to energy waste and noise.

How it works

Simply enter your room sizes, radiator types, and heating system details. Our wizard will calculate the optimal flow settings for each valve.
