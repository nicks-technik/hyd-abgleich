How calculations are made

Calculations are performed per radiator to determine the required water flow. First, we estimate the Room Heat Load ($Q_{room}$) based on area and insulation. Then, we divide this load by the number of radiators in that room ($Q_{rad}$).

Formula: $\text{Flow} (l/h) = \frac{Q_{rad}}{1.163 \times \Delta T}$  •  $\Delta T$ = {{ system.supply_temperature }} - {{ system.return_temperature }} = {{ delta_t }}K
